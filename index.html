Is Bored Guest right for you?

Using it is as “boring” as Bored Room. Setting it up isn't quite: Bored Guest writes visitor records, signed NDAs and photos into your Google Workspace, so someone with admin access has to grant that, and get it right.

A good fit if

  • You have an IT person or an MSP who manages your Google Workspace.
  • They're comfortable granting an app access in the Google Admin console.
  • You want visitor records kept in your own account, under your own security and retention rules.

Hold off if

  • You're the office admin and the IT department, and the Google Admin console is new to you.
  • Nobody at the office has admin rights in Google Workspace.
  • You just need a room sign. That's Bored Room, and anyone can set it up.

Not sure? Forward this page to whoever manages your Google account and let them decide. Or ask us before you buy. We'd rather answer a question than have you stuck with an app you can't finish setting up.

How it works

  1. Buy it

    99¢ on the App Store, or through Apple Business if you deploy iPads in bulk. One copy per lobby iPad.

  2. Sign in with Google

    Pick the Sheet for the visitor log. Hosts come from your Google directory.

  3. Stand it in the lobby

    Lock the iPad to the app. Visitors sign in, hosts get emailed, you keep the audit log.

We don't want your data. Honestly, it sounds “boring.”

A front-desk iPad sees who comes and goes. The only responsible design is one where that never leaves your own accounts.

  • We never see your visitors. The iPad talks to Google and nothing else.
  • Every visit is logged to a Google Sheet in your Drive, for security and audit. You own it; delete it and it's gone.
  • Signed NDAs and photos, if you turn them on, are saved to your Google Drive.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no third-party code.

Why we can say this. There is no Bored Guest backend. We couldn't collect your data if we wanted to, and we've designed it so we never want to.
